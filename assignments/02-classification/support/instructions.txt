Your final score = 0.70 × Performance + 0.20 × Slides + 0.10 × Code

A) PERFORMANCE (weight 70%)
Computed from your results CSV. Two components:

A1) Log-Loss (60% of performance)
For each test observation:
LL_i = -[y_i × log(p_i) + (1-y_i) × log(1-p_i)]
Your average log-loss is compared to an oracle (true model)
and a null baseline (predicts training class rate for all).
Grade = 100 × (LL_null - LL_yours) / (LL_null - LL_oracle)
clamped to [0, 100].

A2) Confidence Intervals (40% of performance)
CI score = 0.6 × coverage + 0.4 × width
Coverage: what fraction of the true probabilities fall within
your 95% CIs (closer to 0.95 is better).
Width: narrower CIs score higher — trivial CIs like [0,1]
receive a low width score.
